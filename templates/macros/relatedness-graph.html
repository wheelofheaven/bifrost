{# Relatedness mini-graph (Decision 15, G3 — see the wheelofheaven repo .claude/plans/strategy-distribution-2026.md §"The graph / relatedness surface"). A scoped view of the content graph: the current entry at the centre, the entries it links to around it, and one further ring of context — the neighbours' own neighbours — drawn small and unlabelled. Reading the shape should tell you something about where the entry sits: a dense mesh means a tightly-bound cluster, a sparse fan means a bridging entry. Geometry is precomputed. The positions come from a force simulation (scripts/build_relatedness_layout.py -> data/relatedness.json), because Tera has neither mutable float loops nor trigonometry. What lands here is already drawable: line segments and points in viewBox coordinates, so this macro only loops and emits SVG. An earlier version laid the neighbours on a fixed coordinate ring, which always read as a star however the edges were drawn, and found its edges by scanning whole sections at render time. Accessibility: the figure is decorative and aria-hidden — the see_also list below it is the semantic, crawlable backbone — so first-ring links are pulled from the tab order. The "full graph" link is deliberately outside the figure so it stays reachable.
